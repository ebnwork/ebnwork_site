Questions, answered

Everything to know before you start.

Is EBN Work only for Kenya?

Yes. Clients and freelancers are based in Kenya, so work, pricing and payments all fit the local market.

How much does it cost to join?

Joining is free. Clients pay no fee, and freelancers pay a service fee only when they get paid.

How is my money protected?

Clients fund the job first, and the money is held until the work is approved, so freelancers are sure of payment and clients only pay for finished work.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Open Get paid, enter the amount and request a withdrawal to your M-Pesa number.

Who runs EBN Work?

EBN Work is a project of the Eldoret Business Network, built to create steady income for local talent and growth for local businesses.
