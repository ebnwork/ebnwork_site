Frequently asked questions

Everything to know before you start.

Who can use EBN Work?

EBN Work is built in Kenya and open to the world. Today you sign up with a Kenyan M-Pesa number. Sign-up for other countries and card payments are coming soon.

How much does it cost to join?

Joining is free. Clients pay no fee, and freelancers pay an 8% service fee only when they get paid.

How is my money protected?

Clients fund the job first, and the money is held until the work is approved, so freelancers are sure of payment and clients only pay for finished work.

How do I withdraw my earnings?

Open Get paid, enter the amount and request a withdrawal to your M-Pesa number.

Who runs EBN Work?

EBN Work is a project of the Eldoret Business Network, built to create steady income for talent and growth for businesses.

Does EBN Work help businesses and SMEs?

Yes. Businesses can hire freelancers, or ask the Eldoret Business Network for websites, branding, bookkeeping set-up and social media management. See our pricing page for typical starting prices, then post a job to request a quote.
