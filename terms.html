Terms of use

1. About EBN Work

EBN Work is an online marketplace operated by the Eldoret Business Network in Eldoret, Kenya. It lets clients post jobs and freelancers submit proposals. EBN Work provides the platform and is not the employer of any freelancer.

2. Accounts

You must be at least 18 years old and give accurate information. You are responsible for your account and for keeping your password safe.

3. Jobs, proposals and contracts

When a client accepts a proposal, a contract is created between the client and the freelancer for the agreed price. Both parties agree to deal honestly and to deliver or pay as agreed.

4. Payments and fees

The client funds the contract through M-Pesa. The money is held until the client approves the delivered work, and is then credited to the freelancer's wallet. Clients pay no additional fee. EBN Work deducts a service fee of 8% from each payment received by a freelancer. Freelancers may request withdrawals to their M-Pesa number, which are processed by EBN Work.

5. Acceptable use

Do not post unlawful, misleading or offensive content, attempt to avoid platform fees on work agreed here, or misuse other people's accounts or information. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.

6. Disputes

If you have a problem with a contract, contact us through the Contact link on the website and we will help the parties reach a fair outcome.

7. Liability

EBN Work does not guarantee the quality of any work or the conduct of any user. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for losses arising from agreements between users.

8. Changes

We may update these terms. Continued use of the platform means you accept the updated terms.

9. Vacancies, listings and adverts

Users may post vacancies and listings for a fee of KES 200 and adverts for a fee of KES 100. Each post stays online for 30 days from payment. Posts must be lawful and honest, must describe what is really offered, and must only use photos you have the right to use. EBN Work does not check posts and is not a party to any sale, job offer or agreement between users. Buyers should see the land, property or goods and check the documents before paying anyone. We may remove any post that breaks these terms or is reported as misleading, and fees are not refunded for posts removed for that reason.

Privacy policy

Information we collect

Your name, email address, phone number, account details, and the jobs, proposals and messages you create on EBN Work. For vacancies, listings and adverts we also keep the photos and text you submit. The contact details and photos you put in a post are shown publicly while it is live.

How we use it

To run your account, connect clients and freelancers, process payments and withdrawals, and send notifications about your jobs and payments, and alerts about new jobs posted on EBN Work. Every job alert has a link to unsubscribe. Your phone number is not shown to other users.

Who else handles it

Payments are processed through Safaricom M-Pesa. Email notifications are sent through an email delivery service. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your account information by contacting us through the Contact link on the website.
